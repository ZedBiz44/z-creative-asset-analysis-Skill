# Response patterns

## One concept or draft
- Recommendation: keep, change a named part, or test a specific alternative.
- Why: audience- and goal-specific reasoning.
- Coverage: actual sources and asset/version inspected.
- Material risk or unknown: only what can affect the decision.
- Next step: exact action; success signal if relevant.

Use a short paragraph and a few bullets for routine requests.
Do not repeat boilerplate cautions in every line.

## New directions
State the audience and one job. Give up to three distinct hook/visual pairs with a payoff.
Recommend one with a short trade-off.
For a creation request, return the Creative Direction Record to production; the VA need not request another handoff.
For a concept-only request, stop with the proposed direction.

## Reference learning
Name source and coverage, observed principle, plausible explanation, suitability for this project, what to adapt and what not to copy.
Accept no useful adaptation. Likes or a supplied “successful” label are not verified causal evidence.

## Image unavailable
“Coverage: written brief only; I could not inspect the image. Based on the brief, [supported direction]. To assess [specific visual question], provide [accessible asset or capability].”
Continue useful concept work. Do not give an execution-readiness verdict.

## Review exposes strategy problem
Answer the precise strategic question, preserve settled choices and return the revised affected part with its reason.
If unresolved after one return, identify the choice for the assignment owner rather than restarting the full workflow.

# Evaluate the direction against its job

Use relevant criteria rather than forcing a checklist onto every graphic.

| Criterion | Decision question | Useful response |
|---|---|---|
| Audience relevance | What known concern, desire or context makes this worth noticing? | Tie the hook to the brief; label assumptions |
| Qualified attention | Who may pause, and are they the intended audience? | Avoid attention that misrepresents the offer |
| Visual meaning | What does the image explain, demonstrate or make concrete? | Specify a meaningful subject or relationship |
| Message clarity | Can the viewer understand the intended point? | Propose concise copy without changing approved facts |
| Curiosity and payoff | What question opens, and where is it answered? | Check image, caption or destination when supplied |
| Trust and proof | What supports a material claim? | Separate real evidence from an illustration |
| Brand attribution | Can the viewer connect the message to the intended business? | Preserve appropriate identity; do not default to a larger logo |
| Context and next step | Does this fit the placement and audience's readiness? | Avoid a universal sales formula |
| Testability | What assumption could a relevant response test? | Suggest one practical signal when useful |

## Distinguish concept and execution

Unreadable text can undermine a good hook: note the marketing implication and route the located craft issue to critique.
Readable text can carry an irrelevant hook: change the direction, not just its colour.
A concept-only assessment cannot establish actual small-screen readability.
A full-size image is not proof of its mobile crop or placement. State the scope inspected.

## Use results carefully

V1 accepts supplied observations; it does not connect analytics accounts or conduct causal campaign analysis.
Keep asset version, audience, placement, period, exposure/spend and baseline attached when available.
A counts comparison without comparable exposure is not proof of a better creative.
Even comparable rates may have targeting, timing or offer differences; report the limit.
For educational content, useful saves or qualified visits may fit. For an offer, enquiries or relevant destination visits may fit. Choose from the actual goal, not a universal likes target.
Mark an unavailable outcome unmeasured. A small or confounded sample may be inconclusive.
Do not claim a design choice caused a lift or promise a future result.

# Social-post alignment acceptance

- Actual finished graphic plus Social Media Post Brief and social post text: inspect the image; give asset-grounded marketing findings and execution findings; name exact versions and limits.
- Actual external reference: identify observed principles and original adaptations; no copying or predicted engagement.
- Request for ten concepts/hooks without an image: assigned content AI follows the shared framework; Analysis does not own ideation and no alternate direction template is introduced.
- Production from a completed Social Media Post Brief: preserve selected concept/hook/text; image then exact headline/branding; review by a different AI; exact export and saved matching text verified.
- Revision: retain working elements, name specific fixes, reconsider after two unsuccessful rounds, distinguish Ready from publishing clearance.
- Other asset such as a logo or photograph: retain general usefulness without requiring social-post paperwork.
- No image access: no claim of visual inspection. No external publication or messages during the pilot.
